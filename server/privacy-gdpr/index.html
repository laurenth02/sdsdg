GENERAL - WHO WE ARE AND WHAT WE DO

The purpose of this Privacy Notice is to explain how our website otkrovenno.online (the Website) and our company, Otkrovenno Media Limited, incorporated in Israel and having its registered address at 1 Otkrovenno Plaza, Tel Aviv, Israel (“Otkrovenno Media” or “we” or “us”) secures, shares and uses personal data that it collects and receives from you through its Website or otherwise. We use the terms “personal data”; “processing”; “controller” and “processor” as they are defined in the General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”).

Otkrovenno Media is an online B2C chat engagement company. It provides its online consumers with an entertainment-based online chatting experience through its Website (the Services). All chatting with our consumers is performed by professional third parties (the Chatters) who have entered into a services agreement with Otkrovenno Media.

It is important to note that Otkrovenno Media itself does not, in principle, view the content of the chats (i.e. messages, audio, images and video) which the consumers engage with our Chatters. Though Otkrovenno Media does have the capability to access the chats, it only makes use of this access if there is a necessary and compelling reason to do so. For instance, for technical troubleshooting or to investigate notifications it has received regarding online chatting behavior which might conflict with the terms of the Website. Outside of such circumstances, Otkrovenno Media does not view the content of the chats in its ordinary course of business.

WHOSE DATA WE PROCESS

We process personal data about different categories of people, such as, in relation to our Services, the personal data of our consumers and, in relation to our general business purposes, the personal data of people who are connected to our suppliers, people who are applying for a job with us and people connected to partners we may enter into business ventures with.

WE ARE YOUR CONTROLLER UNDER GDPR

With respect to the processing of personal data as part of our Services, Otkrovenno Media shall be the Controller under GDPR and the point of contact for all questions and requests regarding the use of personal data. With respect to specifically the processing of any personal data shared by the consumers in the chat exchanges with our Chatters, Otkrovenno Media and the Chatter will together be joint-controllers. However, regardless of this joint-controllership, Otkrovenno Media shall remain the contact for consumers (or other data subjects) for all questions and requests regarding the processing of all their personal data, including any personal data shared in the exchanges with the Chatters.

WHICH DATA WE PROCESS AND WHY

Which data we process and why will depend on the nature of your relationship with us. If you are a consumer, we process your personal data for the purposes of providing our Services and (insofar permissible under law) for sending your direct marketing messages in relation to our own products similar to ones you signed up for. The personal data we use consists of the information you share with us by completing the forms on our Website, but also includes information collected from your device, e.g. IP address, type of device you use.

If you are not a consumer and the nature of your relationship with us falls under the category of general business relations, we will process your data for our general business purposes which may include disclosures to auditors and professional advisors, hiring staff, administering our supplier accounts including managing receivables and payables. The type of personal data processed (unrelated to the Services) will depend on the reason for your interaction with us but will usually include your name and contact details.

LAWFUL BASES FOR PROCESSING

We will only process your personal data if we have a lawful basis for doing so. Depending on the nature of your relationship with us, the lawful basis for the processing of your personal data can include consent, performance of contact, legitimate interests or fulfilment of legal obligations.

  • We rely on ‘performance of contract’ for the use of personal data in order to initiate a contract with you or fulfil our obligations towards you pursuant to our online agreement (mainly the provision of our Services).
  • We rely on consent for use of any special category data that you share with us for use of the Services. This could include sexual preferences which you directly share in the exchanges with the Chatters and which our Chatters use to construct their own reply in an effort to make the chatting experience more engaging for you. You explicitly consent to the use of such information and recognize that if you do not provide consent, then the Services may not be tailored appropriately to your interests.
  • We rely on our ‘legitimate interests’ (i) for sending product information or direct marketing information to our consumers. We use the e-mail address provided to us by the consumer. All marketing information will relate solely to our own products which are similar to the product a consumer signed up for; (ii) and for collecting information on the type of device used (iphone/android/desktop/mobile etc.). We collect this information to get a better understanding on how our consumers are accessing our offers so we can keep that in consideration when optimizing the reach of our offers.
  • We rely on ‘fulfilment of legal obligations’ as the legal basis for collecting personal data in the form of a consumer’s IP address or country (city) of residence. We must collect this information to comply with our legal obligations regarding the proper filing and payment of VAT.

PERSONAL DATA OF CHILDREN

We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from anyone under the age of 18. If you are under 18, please do not visit our website, do not sign up for our Services and please do not send us any of your Personal Data.
