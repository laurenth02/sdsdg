The Right of Withdrawal

Consumers will have a Right of Withdrawal for online purchases of Chats (coins) on the Website (the Product).

This means that a consumer who has purchased a Product shall have a period of 14 days, from the date after the day on which the consumer has purchased the Product, to exercise the Right of Withdrawal without the need to provide any reason for the withdrawal. The consumer can exercise this Right of Withdrawal in the following ways within the 14 day Right of Withdrawal period: (i) simply by sending an e-mail or a letter in which the consumer unequivocally states that he or she likes to exercise the Right of Withdrawal and receive a refund for the purchase of any Product or (ii) by completing the Right of Withdrawal form set out below and emailing this form to info@otkrovenno.online or sending the form by regular mail to Otkrovenno Media’s address (see below).

To exercise your Right of Withdrawal per e-mail, please send your e-mail to info@otkrovenno.online.

To exercise your Right of Withdrawal by regular mail, please send your letter to Otkrovenno Media Limited, registered at 1 Otkrovenno Plaza, Tel Aviv, Israel.

Once you have exercised your Right of Withdrawal, Otkrovenno Media shall provide you with a full refund of the price paid for any purchase of a Product, ultimately within 14 days after we have become aware of your exercise of your Right of Withdrawal. On your end, after your exercise of the Right of Withdrawal, you shall not be able to use any purchased Chat or receive any of the services offered by Otkrovenno Media.
